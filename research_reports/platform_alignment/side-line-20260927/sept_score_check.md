## 1. 计分链复算（平台明细 -> 榜单）：20/20 池逐项一致

- `s_i = |ic_mean| * |icir| * win`（win = 对齐后 RankIC > +0.02 占比）
- `raw_a = 池内 counted 席位的 s_i 算术均值`（不含 factor_value 权重）
- `na = min(raw_a/0.08, 0.7 新池)`, `nb = min(raw_b/0.06, 1)`, `nc = min(max(raw_c,0)/0.6, 1)`
- `comb = 0.2na + 0.35nb + 0.45nc`；`分 = min(comb*40000*1.10, 40000)`
- raw_c 亦逐位复核：`max(annual_rex,0)/max(turn,0.30) * annual_sr * (1-1.2*max_dd)`
