WORKING PAPERS
- Exogenous Distress Risk in China and Asset Pricing Implications (Draft available soon)
 • I review the risk-return dynamics of distress risk under weak-and-strong delisting policy strength,
   with an exogenous delisting policy strengthening event in the Chinese stock market as the structural
   break point. Getting delisted, instead of encountering financial default & bankruptcy events, turns out
   to be the real “disaster” event for the distress risk. Correspondingly, loading on the exposure towards
   delisting policy regime shift risk leads to a positive risk premium before the policy regime shift. This
   systematic risk also contributes to the significant size factor in the Chinese stock market and serves as
   a new mechanism towards reversal anomaly from both time-series and cross-sectional perspectives.
• Seminar presentation: HKU

- Can AI Master Econometrics? Evidence from Econometrics AI Agent on Expert-Level Tasks
  (with Qiang CHEN, Tianyang HAN, Jin LI, Ye LUO, Xiaowei ZHANG and Tuo ZHOU) (Available
  on arXiv)
• We develop an Econometrics AI Agent built on the open-source MetaGPT framework, applying
   prompt engineering methods to embed AI agent system with solid applied econometrics domain
   knowledge. The domain-specialized AI agent significantly outperforms both benchmark LLMs and
   general-purpose AI agents under comparative tests. This work establishes a testbed for exploring AI's
   impact on social science research and enables cost-effective integration of domain expertise.
• Seminar presentation: HKU, University of Macau, Fudan University, Shandong University
