### Independent information and combinations

The correlation analysis was extended from the initial seven statistical directions to an exhaustive pair/triple test. The best 10-day low-correlation candidate at the main `0.80` threshold is the three-factor combination `VERIFY10-E260910-04 + SIZE-ONLY-20260911 + H03-T10-SINGLE`, with training/validation/full-period net excess of `20.14% / 17.02% / 18.92%`. A simpler `SIZE-ONLY + H03-T10-SINGLE` combination remains the more interpretable candidate and improves over its strongest component in the validation comparison. Five-day combinations did not beat the strongest single-factor baseline in the full-period comparison.

### AlphaPROBE and net-excess-aware mining

AlphaPROBE GP/GFlowNet/mainline runs now share the verified-field policy and the alignment rule version. GFlowNet supports a bounded train-only net-excess reward, while validation and test windows remain evaluation-only. The completed net-reward run found promising individual candidates, but the ensemble net excess remained negative after turnover costs, so the result supports single-factor screening rather than automatic ensemble deployment.

- Platform net excess is a screening field; local five-year and recent values can be negative even when the platform value is positive.
- Proxy fields are labeled in the source reports and are not byte-level equivalents of PandaAI internals.
- Search and combination results are historical research records, not investment advice or proof of future performance.
